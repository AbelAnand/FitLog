Help and support

Answers to common questions, and how to get in touch.

Get in touch. Report a problem or ask a question on the FitLog issue tracker. Posts there are public, so leave out anything private. Say which iPhone and iOS version you use, and what you did just before the problem.

For a full walk-through of the app, see How to use FitLog.

Your data

Where are my workouts stored?

On your iPhone, inside the app. FitLog has no accounts and no server. See the privacy policy.

I got a new iPhone. How do I bring my workouts?

Follow Moving to a new iPhone. In short: save a backup file on the old phone first, then restore it on the new one if the workouts did not arrive by themselves.

I deleted FitLog, or lost my iPhone. Can my workouts be recovered?

Only from a copy you made: a FitLog backup file, a spreadsheet export, or an iPhone backup from before the loss. The developer has no copy and cannot recover it for you.

The restore says my file is not a FitLog backup

Pick the file that FitLog saved, whose name starts with fitlog-backup and ends in .json, or a spreadsheet that FitLog exported, ending in .csv. Files that were edited by hand or made by another app may not be readable.

How do I delete a set or a workout?

Swipe it to the left. A short swipe shows a Delete button, and a long swipe deletes it straight away. You can also hold a workout to see its options.

How do I delete everything?

Open Settings, then Erase everything, and confirm.

Using the app

How is a personal record decided?

A set is marked PR when its weight is higher than the heaviest you have logged for that exercise in any earlier workout. Warm-up sets do not count.

How does the streak work?

A week counts when you train on at least as many different days as your weekly goal. Weeks run Monday to Sunday. You can change the goal in Settings.

Reminders are not showing up

Check that notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings, then Notifications, then FitLog, and that reminders are switched on in the app's Settings. A Focus mode can also silence them.

The widget shows old numbers

The widget updates when you open FitLog. Open the app once and return to the Home Screen.

FitLog says it couldn't open my log

Close FitLog completely and open it again. If the message returns, free up storage space on the iPhone and restart it. Your earlier workouts are still stored. If it keeps happening, get in touch before deleting the app, as deleting it removes the log.

About FitLog

Is FitLog really free?

Yes. There are no subscriptions, no ads and no locked features.

Is there a version for Android or the web?

No. FitLog is an iPhone app.
